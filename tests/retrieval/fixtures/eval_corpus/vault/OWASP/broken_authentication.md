Broken Authentication
=====================

Version: 1.0 (Draft)

Broken authentication vulnerabilities allow attackers to compromise passwords, session tokens, or exploit other implementation flaws to assume other users' identities. Common causes include permitting weak or default passwords, exposing session identifiers in URLs, not invalidating session tokens on logout, and failing to implement protections against credential stuffing or brute-force login attempts. Multi-factor authentication and rate-limiting on login endpoints are the most effective mitigations.
